# Protected proxy passwords

SOCKS5 proxy passwords are out of the config file. They used to sit in plain text in `config/steinconnect.json`, readable by anyone who opened the file; now they are kept apart, protected by Windows, and only open on your account, on this computer.

- **No more plain-text passwords**: the password of the proxy in use and those of the saved proxies go to `config/steinconnect.secrets`, encrypted by Windows. Copied to another computer the file is useless: just type the password again there.
- **Automatic migration**: the first time the game starts with this version, the passwords that were in `steinconnect.json` move to the protected place and disappear from the file. Nothing to do, and the Connect screen looks the same.
- **Settings saved without rushing**: typing in a field no longer writes the file on every key; it is saved right after you stop, and is never left half-written if the game closes midway.
- **Under the hood**: the mod no longer talks to Minecraft directly — keys, chat, screens, players and inventory go through Stein Loader.
- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.23**: update with the installer before the mod.
