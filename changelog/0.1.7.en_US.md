# Connect 0.1.7: unlimited accounts

The Connect account list has no limit anymore and no longer reorders on every click.

- **Unlimited accounts**: the offline nick list no longer stops at 6 — some people use dozens of accounts.
- **The list stays put**: logging in with an account already on the list no longer moves it to the top; only a new account goes on top.
- Saved proxies still go up to 6.
